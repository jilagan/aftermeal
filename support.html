Common questions

Where is my diary stored?
Only on your iPhone. There is no account and no server.

How do I move my diary to a new iPhone?
Restore the new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of the old one. The diary comes with the backup.

How do I share my diary with my doctor?
Settings, then Doctor visit report (PDF). It covers the last 30 days. You can also export everything as JSON or CSV.

Why don't I see food patterns yet?
Patterns need at least 10 days with both meals and symptoms logged on the same or next day.

A food I eat isn't in the database.
Type it and choose "Add as typed". It is saved as written. Email us the name and we'll consider adding it.

How do I delete everything?
Settings, then Delete all data.

Medical note

Aftermeal is a personal diary and not medical advice. Get medical care right away for heavy bleeding, high fever, severe or worsening belly pain, repeated vomiting, or signs of dehydration.
