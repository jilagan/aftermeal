Privacy Policy

Aftermeal: IBD Food Diary · Archmob Pte Ltd · Effective 20 November 2026

Summary

Aftermeal stores your diary on your iPhone only. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no server, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

The meals, symptoms, medicines, notes and settings you enter are saved in the app's storage on your iPhone. They never leave your iPhone unless you export or share them yourself.

What we collect

Nothing. Archmob does not operate a server for Aftermeal and cannot see your diary.

Backups

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, your diary is part of that device backup. Apple handles device backups under your Apple account settings.

Exports and reports

You can export your diary as a PDF report, a JSON file or a CSV file. The file is created on your iPhone and goes only where you send it, for example to your doctor by email. Once shared, the copy is handled by whoever receives it.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings, then Delete all data. Deleting the app also deletes the diary from your iPhone.

Health information

Aftermeal is a personal diary. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any disease, and it does not replace advice from your doctor. Aftermeal does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Children

Aftermeal is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example by adding an optional research program, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and any data sharing will require your explicit consent in the app.
