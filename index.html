Aftermeal

IBD food and symptom diary for iPhone

Aftermeal is a diary for people with Crohn's disease and ulcerative colitis. Log meals from a food database, track stool type, pain, blood and urgency, keep a record of your medicines, and see which foods came before your rough days.

  • Search a database of home dishes and restaurant foods, or type your own.
  • Symptom check-ins with the Bristol Stool Scale.
  • Daily medicine checklist, plus last dose and next due date for injections and infusions.
  • 14-day trend and food patterns from your own logs.
  • A PDF report of the last 30 days to bring to your doctor.
  • No account. Your diary stays on your iPhone.

Aftermeal is a personal diary and not medical advice. Made by Archmob Pte Ltd.
